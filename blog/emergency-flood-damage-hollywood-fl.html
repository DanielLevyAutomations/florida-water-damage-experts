Why Hollywood, FL Is at High Risk for Flooding

Hollywood, Florida sits in one of the most flood-prone areas of Broward County. Several factors combine to create serious flood risk:

  • Low elevation — much of Hollywood sits just 3–6 feet above sea level, making it highly vulnerable to storm surge and rising water tables
  • Coastal exposure — Hollywood's beachfront location exposes it directly to tropical storms and hurricane storm surge
  • Aging drainage infrastructure — parts of Hollywood's stormwater system struggle to handle the heavy downpours common in South Florida
  • High water table — groundwater levels in Hollywood are often just a few feet below the surface, reducing the ground's ability to absorb rainwater
  • King tides — seasonal high tides can push water up through storm drains and canals into low-lying neighborhoods

Immediate Steps When Your Hollywood Home Floods

Step 1: Prioritize Safety

Flood water is dangerous. It can contain sewage, chemicals, and bacteria. Never walk through standing flood water if you can avoid it. If water has reached electrical outlets, do not enter the area until power is shut off. If the flooding is severe, evacuate and call 911.

Step 2: Shut Off Utilities

If you can safely access them, turn off electricity at the main breaker and gas at the meter. Do not touch electrical equipment while standing in water.

Step 3: Call for Professional Help

Flood damage requires immediate professional intervention. The contaminated water, structural saturation, and mold risk make this far beyond a DIY cleanup. Call a licensed restoration company that can respond within the hour.

Step 4: Document the Damage

Once it's safe, use your phone to photograph and video everything: water levels, damaged walls, floors, furniture, appliances, and personal belongings. This documentation is critical for both insurance claims and FEMA assistance if applicable.

Step 5: Remove Valuables

If you can safely do so, move important documents, electronics, and irreplaceable items to dry ground. Lift furniture legs off wet carpet by placing aluminum foil or plastic underneath.

Step 6: Do NOT Turn On the HVAC

If your HVAC system was exposed to flood water, do not turn it on. Running a contaminated HVAC system will spread bacteria and mold spores throughout your entire home.

The Flood Damage Restoration Process

Professional flood damage restoration in Hollywood follows an intensive process:

Emergency Water Extraction

Industrial pumps and truck-mounted extraction units remove standing water as fast as possible. The faster water is removed, the more of your home can be saved. In flood situations, this often involves removing thousands of gallons.

Contamination Assessment

Flood water is classified as Category 3 (black water) — the most hazardous type. It may contain sewage, chemicals, pesticides, and harmful bacteria. Technicians assess which materials can be salvaged and which must be removed for health safety.

Demolition of Unsalvageable Materials

Drywall that has absorbed flood water must typically be removed at least 12 inches above the water line. Contaminated carpet, padding, and insulation are removed and disposed of properly.

Structural Drying and Dehumidification

Commercial dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers dry the structural components of your home — studs, subfloor, concrete, and remaining drywall. This process typically takes 3–5 days, monitored daily with moisture meters.

Antimicrobial Treatment

All surfaces exposed to flood water are treated with professional-grade antimicrobial solutions to kill bacteria and prevent mold growth.

Mold Prevention

In Hollywood's climate, mold can begin growing within 24–48 hours of flooding. Preventive treatment during the drying phase is essential. Post-drying air quality testing confirms the environment is safe.

Reconstruction

Once the structure is completely dry and treated, reconstruction begins: new drywall, flooring, baseboards, paint, and any other materials that were removed. The goal is to return your home to its pre-flood condition.

Flood Insurance in Hollywood, FL

Critical information for Hollywood homeowners:

  • Standard homeowner's insurance does NOT cover flood damage. You need a separate flood insurance policy, typically through the National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP).
  • If you have flood insurance, file your claim as soon as possible. Document everything with photos and video before any cleanup begins.
  • After a declared disaster, FEMA may provide assistance even without flood insurance, but this is typically loans, not grants.
  • A professional restoration company can provide the detailed documentation your insurance company needs to process your claim.
Many Hollywood neighborhoods are in FEMA-designated flood zones. If you don't currently have flood insurance, get a policy now. There is typically a 30-day waiting period before coverage begins.

How to Prepare for Flood Season in Hollywood

  • Purchase flood insurance if you don't have it — don't wait for a storm warning
  • Know your evacuation routes and have an emergency kit ready
  • Install a sump pump with battery backup if your home has a history of flooding
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear to direct water away from your foundation
  • Elevate critical utilities (water heater, electrical panel, HVAC) above potential flood levels when possible
  • Store important documents in a waterproof safe or digitize them
  • Have a restoration company's number saved in your phone before you need it: (954) 701-4702
