Why Water Damage Is So Common in Hollywood, FL

Hollywood is the second-largest city in Broward County, with more than 153,000 residents spread across neighborhoods that range from beachfront high-rises to inland single-family communities. That diversity means water damage takes many forms here, and almost no neighborhood is immune.

The beachside areas — primarily zip codes 33019 and 33020 — face the highest risk from coastal flooding. Storm surge, king tides, and heavy tropical rainfall regularly push water into properties along the Broadwalk and Hollywood Beach. These areas sit in FEMA-designated flood zones where even moderate storms can cause significant water intrusion.

Inland neighborhoods like Hollywood Hills (33021), Emerald Hills (33021), and the western communities in 33023 and 33024 face different but equally serious challenges. Many homes in these areas were built in the 1960s and 1970s with plumbing systems that have been deteriorating for decades. Cast iron drain lines corrode from the inside out, and polybutylene supply lines become brittle and crack without warning.

Hollywood's proximity to the C-10 canal, the South Fork of the New River, and the Intracoastal Waterway creates additional flood exposure during heavy rain events. When these waterways rise, low-lying streets and properties take the hit first. If your Hollywood home has experienced any water intrusion, contact us for emergency help before the damage spreads.

What to Do When Water Damage Hits Your Hollywood Home

1

Shut Off the Water Source

If the water is coming from a pipe, appliance, or fixture, turn off the water supply immediately. If you can't locate the source, shut off the main water valve to your home. Avoid entering rooms with standing water if there is any risk of electrical contact.

2

Call Your Insurance Company, Then Call Us

Report the damage to your insurance company as soon as possible. Then call us at (954) 701-4702. We'll dispatch a crew to your Hollywood home immediately and coordinate directly with your insurance adjuster.

3

Protect What You Can

Move valuables, electronics, and important documents to a dry area. Take photos and video of all damage before anything is moved or cleaned — your insurance company will need this documentation.

!

Don't Try to Dry It Yourself

Household fans and open windows are not enough for structural drying in Hollywood's humid climate. Incomplete drying leads to mold growth inside walls and under flooring that costs thousands more to fix later. Learn more in our guide to water damage repair in South Florida.

Hollywood Water Damage FAQ

How fast can you reach my Hollywood home?+
We can typically reach Hollywood properties within 30 to 50 minutes. Our crews are positioned throughout Broward County and respond 24/7, including weekends and holidays.
I have a condo on Hollywood Beach — do you work with condo associations?+
Yes. We regularly work in Hollywood Beach condos and coordinate directly with condo associations and property managers to ensure all repairs meet building requirements and HOA standards. We handle the coordination so you don't have to.
Is hurricane damage covered by homeowner's insurance?+
Wind damage from hurricanes is typically covered by your homeowner's insurance policy. However, flood damage requires a separate flood insurance policy. Many Hollywood homeowners don't realize this distinction until it's too late. We recommend reviewing your coverage before hurricane season.
How can I prevent water damage during hurricane season?+
Get a roof inspection before hurricane season, clean your gutters and downspouts, know where your main water shut-off valve is, and make sure your sump pump is working. Consider flood barriers if you're in a low-lying area of Hollywood. Trim trees near your home and secure outdoor items that could become projectiles.
Does Hollywood FL have a mold problem?+
Yes. Hollywood's year-round humidity levels above 70% create ideal conditions for mold growth. Any water intrusion — even a small leak — can lead to mold within 24 to 48 hours if not properly dried. Regular inspection and prompt response to any water issues are essential.
What if I already see mold in my Hollywood home?+
Don't disturb it. Disturbing mold can release spores into the air and spread contamination to other areas of your home. Call us for a professional mold inspection and remediation plan. We'll contain the affected area, remove the mold safely, and treat surfaces to prevent regrowth.
