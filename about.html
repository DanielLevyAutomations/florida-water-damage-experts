About Florida Water Damage Experts

Locally owned. Fully licensed. Trusted by thousands of Broward County homeowners since 2010. We're your neighbors, and we're here when disaster strikes.

Our Story

Florida Water Damage Experts was founded with a simple mission: be the company we'd want to call if our own home was flooding at 2 AM. That means answering the phone immediately, arriving fast, doing the job right, and treating every homeowner like family.

Over 15 years later, we've restored more than 5,000 homes across Broward County. From a burst pipe in a Fort Lauderdale condo to hurricane flooding in Hollywood, we've handled every type of water emergency South Florida can throw at us.

We're not a franchise. We're not a national chain. We're a locally owned Broward County company staffed by people who live in the same communities we serve. When we restore your home, we're protecting our own neighborhood.
