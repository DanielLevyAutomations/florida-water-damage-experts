Common Water Damage Causes in Coral Springs

Heavy Rainfall & Poor Drainage

Coral Springs' inland location and flat terrain make it susceptible to standing water during heavy rains. Overwhelmed storm drains push floodwater toward homes and garages.

Water Heater & Appliance Failures

Aging water heaters, washing machines, and dishwashers are a top cause of indoor flooding in Coral Springs homes. A single appliance failure can damage multiple rooms.

Slab Leaks & Foundation Shifts

Coral Springs homes built on concrete slabs are vulnerable to underground pipe leaks that go unnoticed, causing hidden damage to flooring and walls over time.
