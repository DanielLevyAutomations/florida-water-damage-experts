Mold Remediation in Broward County: The Complete Homeowner's Guide

By Florida Water Damage Experts • Updated March 2026 • 10 min read

If you can see mold growing in your Broward County home — or if you smell that unmistakable musty odor — you have a problem that won't resolve itself. Mold spreads aggressively in South Florida's warm, humid climate, and every day you wait means more contamination, more health risk, and higher remediation costs.

Why Mold Is a Major Problem in Broward County

Broward County is one of the most mold-prone regions in the United States. Here's why:

  • Year-round humidity — Broward County's average humidity exceeds 70%, creating ideal conditions for mold growth in homes
  • Warm temperatures — mold thrives between 77°F and 86°F, which describes most of the year in South Florida
  • Frequent water intrusion — heavy rains, hurricanes, flooding, plumbing failures, and AC leaks provide the moisture mold needs to colonize
  • Older building stock — many Broward County homes were built before modern moisture barriers and ventilation standards

Common Signs of Mold in Your Home

Mold isn't always visible. Watch for these warning signs:

  • Visible mold growth — black, green, white, or gray patches on walls, ceilings, baseboards, or in bathrooms
  • Musty smell — a persistent earthy or damp odor, especially in enclosed spaces, closets, or rooms with poor ventilation
  • Water stains — discoloration on walls or ceilings often indicates past or ongoing moisture problems where mold may be growing behind the surface
  • Health symptoms — unexplained allergies, respiratory issues, headaches, or eye irritation that improve when you leave the home
  • Peeling or bubbling paint — moisture behind walls causes paint to peel, bubble, or warp, often indicating hidden mold
  • Warped or buckled flooring — moisture under flooring can cause warping and often harbors mold underneath

The Health Risks of Mold Exposure

Mold is not just a cosmetic problem. Exposure to mold spores can cause serious health issues, especially for vulnerable individuals:

  • Allergic reactions including sneezing, runny nose, red eyes, and skin rashes
  • Asthma attacks and worsening of existing respiratory conditions
  • Chronic sinus infections
  • Headaches, fatigue, and difficulty concentrating
  • More severe reactions in children, the elderly, and immunocompromised individuals
The CDC and EPA both recommend professional remediation for any mold growth larger than 10 square feet. In Broward County's climate, even smaller areas of mold should be taken seriously because they can spread rapidly.

The Professional Mold Remediation Process

Effective mold remediation is not the same as simply cleaning mold off a surface. Here's what a proper remediation involves:

1. Inspection and Testing

Certified technicians inspect your entire home, including areas you can't easily see — behind walls, inside HVAC systems, under flooring. Air quality testing identifies the type and concentration of mold spores.

2. Containment

Before any mold is disturbed, the affected area is sealed with plastic sheeting and negative air pressure to prevent spores from spreading to clean areas of your home.

3. Air Filtration

HEPA air scrubbers and negative air machines capture microscopic mold spores from the air throughout the remediation process.

4. Mold Removal

Depending on the extent of contamination, this may involve removing drywall, insulation, carpet, or other materials that can't be salvaged. Hard surfaces are scrubbed and treated with antimicrobial solutions.

5. Cleaning and Sanitizing

All affected areas and belongings are cleaned. HEPA vacuuming removes settled spores from every surface. Antimicrobial treatments prevent regrowth.

6. Moisture Elimination

The source of moisture that caused the mold must be identified and fixed. Without eliminating the moisture source, mold will return. This may involve plumbing repairs, improved ventilation, or waterproofing.

7. Restoration

Removed drywall, flooring, and other materials are replaced. Your home is returned to pre-mold condition.

Mold Remediation Costs in Broward County

Costs depend on the size of the affected area, the type of mold, and the materials involved:

  • Small area (less than 50 sq ft): $1,500–$3,500
  • Medium area (50–200 sq ft): $3,500–$10,000
  • Large area (full rooms, HVAC systems, structural): $10,000–$30,000+

Important: mold remediation is sometimes covered by homeowner's insurance if the mold resulted from a covered water damage event. Documentation from a professional restoration company is critical for insurance claims.

Florida Mold Remediation Laws

Florida has specific regulations governing mold remediation:

  • Companies performing mold remediation must hold a valid Florida mold remediation license
  • Mold assessors and mold remediators must be different entities to prevent conflicts of interest
  • Post-remediation verification testing should confirm that the mold has been successfully removed

Always verify that any company you hire holds a current Florida mold remediation license. Florida Water Damage Experts is fully licensed for mold remediation in the state of Florida.

Preventing Mold in Your Broward County Home

  • Keep indoor humidity below 60% using dehumidifiers or your AC system
  • Fix any water leaks immediately, no matter how small
  • Ensure bathrooms, kitchens, and laundry rooms are properly ventilated
  • Service your HVAC system regularly and keep drain lines clear
  • Inspect your home after any storm or heavy rainfall for signs of water intrusion
  • Never ignore musty smells — investigate immediately
  • Consider annual professional mold inspections given Broward County's climate
