Common Water Damage Causes in Fort Lauderdale

South Florida's climate creates unique risks for homeowners.

Hurricane & Tropical Storm Flooding

Fort Lauderdale sits at sea level with a high water table. Storm surge and heavy rainfall regularly overwhelm drainage systems and flood homes.

Aging Plumbing & Pipe Failures

Many Fort Lauderdale homes have aging polybutylene or copper pipes prone to bursting. A single pipe failure can release hundreds of gallons per hour.

AC Condensation & Leaks

Air conditioners run year-round in South Florida. Clogged drain lines and failed condensation pans are a leading cause of hidden water damage in Fort Lauderdale homes.
